How it works

Three taps, no app switching.

Jawab is a keyboard, so it lives where the orders already are: inside the WhatsApp chat.

1

Open the Jawab keyboard

In any customer chat, switch to the Jawab keyboard. Text order or voice note, both work.

2

Get a ready reply

Jawab drafts a polite reply in the customer's language and script, using only the prices you saved.

3

Send and pack

Tap send. The items go straight into a pick list for your delivery boy.

Features

Built for a busy counter.

अ

Speaks your customer's language

Hinglish, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati and Bengali. It replies in the same language and script the customer used.

₹

Your prices, nothing made up

Replies only use prices from your own list. If an item isn't listed, Jawab asks you instead of guessing.

🎙

Understands voice notes

Customers who send a voice note get the same quick, written reply and a clean order list.

✓

Never over-promises

Jawab won't offer refunds, discounts or delivery times you haven't set. You stay in control of every message.
